SAMPLE JOB DESCRIPTION - Data Analyst (fictional, for demonstration only)

We are hiring a Data Analyst to turn business data into clear insights for decision makers.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean and analyze large datasets from multiple sources.
- Build interactive dashboards and reports in Power BI or Tableau.
- Write SQL queries to extract and aggregate data.
- Perform statistical analysis and A/B testing to evaluate business experiments.
- Present findings to stakeholders in a clear and concise way.

Requirements:
- Strong SQL skills and advanced Excel.
- Experience with Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data analysis.
- Knowledge of statistics and data visualization best practices.
- Excellent communication and attention to detail.

Nice to have:
- Experience with ETL pipelines.
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts.
